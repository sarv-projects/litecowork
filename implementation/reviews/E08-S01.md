## Source slice

`CoworkerSettings` now fences roster, detail refresh, primary-selection, revision, and
status responses to the Workspace request epoch that admitted them. A response from a
previous Workspace cannot overwrite the newly selected Workspace view. Paginated roster
append validates that every item belongs to the selected Workspace, de-duplicates stable
Coworker IDs, and rejects a cursor that does not advance. Quick Create starts with no
implicit purpose and requires both a name and a short plain-language purpose. Edit/status
semantics are unchanged; this slice creates, selects, or starts no Conversation.

The implementation is in `apps/litecowork-ui/src/coworkers/CoworkerSettings.tsx` and
`apps/litecowork-ui/src/coworkers/coworker-roster-request-epoch.ts`. Focused pure tests are
in `apps/litecowork-ui/tests/coworker-roster-request-epoch.test.ts`.

## Same-Workspace Coworker switching and receipt identity fences — 2026-10-17

Coworker detail and presence reads now bind both the selected Workspace and selected
Coworker identity, including a per-detail generation for same-identity refresh races.
Late responses cannot replace another Coworker's settings, presence, or loading/error state.
Revision/status responses validate their returned Coworker identity before updating the
view; a confirmed but stale selection response clears only its exact request retry key.
Workspace-primary receipts validate their Workspace identity before updating the local
projection. Switching roster selection clears only Coworker-specific busy state, while an
in-flight Workspace-primary action remains serialized.

This pure helper/API-level evidence does not mount the React page or prove packaged
