- `a_stale_routine_head_is_refused_without_writing_a_task` — after the Routine head moves

- `routine_inputs_that_do_not_materialize_are_refused_without_a_task` — a revision whose

  is `Invalid` and writes no Task or receipt.
- `a_foreign_principal_cannot_replay_a_routine_task_receipt` — a different principal
  reusing the owner's request key is refused and cannot read the owner receipt.
