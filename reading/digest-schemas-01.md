## Cross-cutting observations
- Versioning is consistent in shape across artifacts: `schema_version` const 1 on envelopes, `.vN` suffix on event types, `payload_version` on stream items, `renderer_contract_version` separate from document `schema_version`. Only `resource.created`, `verification.completed`, `channel.host.assignment.changed`, `resource.context_document.purge.acknowledged`, `conversation.turn.created` have v2.
- Digest discipline repeats: `sha256:[a-f0-9]{64}` for payloads, slices, artifacts, package assets, presentation documents; `semantic_content_digest` distinct from `document_digest`.
- "Unknown is not success" is encoded structurally: `EFFECT_AMBIGUOUS`, `save_result.status: UNKNOWN`, `availability: INTEGRITY_FAILED`, `freshness: UNKNOWN|STALE`.
- Byte-based text addressing (`start_utf8_byte` / `end_utf8_byte_exclusive`) appears in `slice`, `locator.TEXT_SPAN` — text is referenced, not embedded.
- Duplication risk: `rich-presentation` `block` enum is restated in `operator-stream` `block_kind` and again in `draft_event.payload.block_kind`; `resource_ref` is redefined in 4 files with different required fields (`workspace_id` present in delegation/stream, absent in rich-presentation).
- Cloud vocabulary (`CLOUD_PREFERRED`, `CLOUD_ONLY`, `RUNTIME_STATUS`) persists in V1-local schemas — scope drift vs the "V1 is local-first desktop" invariant.
